Privacy Policy & Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: 17 September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KKF HD Status. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Media Processing & Local Data

KKF HD Status is a utility application designed to optimize videos for status updates. All video compression happens locally on your device. We do not upload, view, or store your photos or videos on our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not require user accounts. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect anonymous data:

  • Firebase: For analytics and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements.
  • RevenueCat: For managing premium subscriptions securely.
  • Meta WhatsApp API: Used strictly for customer support if you contact us.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

Because we do not use accounts, you can delete your app data at any time by going to your device's Settings > Apps > KKF HD Status > Storage and clicking "Clear Data", or by uninstalling the app.

Support Data: If you contacted our WhatsApp Support and want your chat history deleted, please send a message saying "DELETE MY DATA" to our support number, or email support@kkfasil.com. It will be erased within 24 hours.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at support@kkfasil.com.
